Storyline
The film follows Rudhra (Mahesh Babu), a Shiva devotee, on a perilous quest across time to uncover a cosmic artifact of immense power.

The narrative spans multiple yugas (Treta to Kali) and continents, including Africa and Antarctica.

Mahesh Babu will also appear as Lord Rama in a special Ramayana-inspired sequence.

Priyanka Chopra’s character, Mandakini, plays a pivotal role in Rudhra’s journey.

Deals:  

Scale & Budget: At ₹1,400 crore, it surpasses RRR and Baahubali in ambition.

Global Appeal: Shot across continents, including Antarctica, making it a landmark in Indian cinema.

IMAX Experience: Designed for premium large-format viewing, ensuring breathtaking visuals.

Star Power: First collaboration between Mahesh Babu and SS Rajamouli, with Priyanka Chopra returning to Telugu cinema after decades.
